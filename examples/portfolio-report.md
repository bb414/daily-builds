# Assurance mix: sharing one budget across an AI inventory

Simplified and illustrative, not legal advice. Costs, durations and coverages are illustrative judgements.

Budget £250k across 8 systems, appetite 8.0.

## How the approaches compare

Optimised puts first bringing as many systems as possible within appetite, then the lowest total of their worst scores. Riskiest first funds the riskiest system's cheapest plan within appetite, then the next; the two share-based rules split the budget and buy the lowest-risk plan each share affords.

| Approach | Systems within appetite | Average worst score | Spent |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Optimised | 6 of 8 | 7.5 | £247k |
| Riskiest first | 4 of 8 | 10.0 | £236k |
| Shares by inherent risk | 3 of 8 | 7.7 | £233k |
| Equal shares | 4 of 8 | 7.8 | £242k |
