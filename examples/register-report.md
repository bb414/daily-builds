## Duties by system

### Shortlister: CV screening for a retail chain

High-risk (Annex III use), as deployer.

- Take measures to support the AI literacy of staff and others who operate or use the system for you (Art 4), from 2 February 2025
- Use the system in line with the provider's instructions (Art 26(1)), from 2 December 2027
- Give human oversight to people with the competence, training and authority to do it (Art 26(2)), from 2 December 2027
- Make sure input data you control is relevant and sufficiently representative (Art 26(4)), from 2 December 2027
- Monitor how it operates, and tell the provider and authorities about risks and serious incidents (Art 26(5)), from 2 December 2027
- Keep the automatically generated logs for at least six months (Art 26(6)), from 2 December 2027
- Inform workers' representatives and affected workers before using it at work (Art 26(7)), from 2 December 2027
- Tell people when they are subject to decisions the system makes or helps to make (Art 26(11)), from 2 December 2027
- Be ready to explain the system's role in a decision to an affected person who asks (Art 86), from 2 December 2027

### Helpdesk chatbot on a shop website

Transparency duties, as provider and deployer.

- Take measures to support the AI literacy of staff and others who operate or use the system for you (Art 4), from 2 February 2025
- Make sure people are told they are interacting with an AI system, unless that is obvious (Art 50(1)), from 2 August 2026
- Mark generated audio, images, video and text in a machine-readable way so they can be detected as AI-generated (Art 50(2)), from 2 December 2026

### Mood monitor for call-centre staff

Banned practice, as deployer.

- Stop: emotion recognition in a workplace or educational institution, other than for medical or safety reasons (Art 5(1)(f)), from 2 February 2025
- Take measures to support the AI literacy of staff and others who operate or use the system for you (Art 4), from 2 February 2025

### Insulin dosing app (a medical device)

High-risk (product under Annex I), as provider.

- Take measures to support the AI literacy of staff and others who operate or use the system for you (Art 4), from 2 February 2025
- Make sure people are told they are interacting with an AI system, unless that is obvious (Art 50(1)), from 2 August 2026
- Run a risk management system across the whole life cycle (Art 9), from 2 August 2028
- Govern training, validation and testing data, including checks for bias (Art 10), from 2 August 2028
- Draw up technical documentation (a simplified form is allowed for SMEs and small mid-caps) (Art 11, Annex IV), from 2 August 2028
- Build in automatic logging of events (Art 12), from 2 August 2028
- Give deployers clear instructions for use (Art 13), from 2 August 2028
- Design the system so people can oversee it effectively (Art 14), from 2 August 2028
- Achieve appropriate accuracy, robustness and cybersecurity (Art 15), from 2 August 2028
- Operate a quality management system (proportionate for SMEs and small mid-caps) (Art 17), from 2 August 2028
- Keep documentation for 10 years and keep the logs under your control (Arts 18 and 19), from 2 August 2028
- Complete the conformity assessment, sign the EU declaration of conformity and affix the CE marking (Arts 43, 47 and 48), from 2 August 2028
- Take corrective action, and inform others, if the system stops conforming (Art 20), from 2 August 2028
- Monitor the system after it is placed on the market (Art 72), from 2 August 2028
- Report serious incidents to the market surveillance authority (Art 73), from 2 August 2028
