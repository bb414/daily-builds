Which single fix helps a pilots-first organisation most?

Each bar applies one governance practice on its own and leaves everything else unchanged. In this run the biggest single gain comes from add explicit stop criteria (+£3.2m net value, down 6 points in the share of pilots scaled). Notice that it actually scales fewer pilots. The gain comes from no longer paying, quarter after quarter, for pilots that were never going to make it.
