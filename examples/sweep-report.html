Reliance lab: where does human review pay?

The reliance model run across 3,888 combinations of assumptions: two accuracies, how often the person and the AI get the same cases wrong, how much each side's confidence tells you, and whether either is overconfident. "Beats both" means the team is more than 0.5 points above the better of the person and the AI alone.
