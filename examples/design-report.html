How long before skill shows?

Each scoring rule simulated 400 times at seven lengths, from one week to a year of trading days. "Most skilled team wins" is how often the team with the highest true Sharpe ratio finishes first. Rank correlation measures how closely the whole final table follows true skill: 0 is pure luck, 1 is perfectly.

What the numbers say

  • Ranking on Sharpe ratio picked out the most skilled team most often at 7 of the 7 lengths tested.
  • Over 1 year, the most skilled team won only 27% of challenges under the best rule, and final places matched true skill with a rank correlation of 0.64 (1 would be perfect).
  • Over four weeks, gamblers won 69% of return-ranked challenges against 17% ranked on Sharpe ratio, and the winner had no real edge in 34% of return-ranked ones.
