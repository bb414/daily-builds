Bank the saving as a headcount cut?

The pilot's business case frees 1.8 people at Assess claim; in practice the AI frees 0.9. Cut 2 of 5 people: cases take 22.3 days on average (+20.3 days against no AI, +20.4 days against keeping everyone). Assess claim would be 100% busy and 0.0% of cases close within 5 days; its queue keeps growing.

How robust is it?

With 10 runs of different luck, the AI made cases faster in 10 and slower in 0. The change ranged from −2.0 h to −57 min (average −1.2 h).
