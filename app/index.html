How well do you spot scams, with and without an AI checker?

You'll play Sam, a university student, and decide whether 28 messages are scams or genuine: texts, emails, direct messages and more. Halfway through, an AI checker gives you a second opinion. It is right most of the time, but not always.

  1. 1

    On your own

    8 messages. Judge each one yourself.

  2. 2

    With the checker

    12 messages. Answer first, see what the checker says, then give your final answer.

  3. 3

    On your own again

    8 more messages, without the checker. Did round 2 help or hinder?

Takes about 10 minutes. There is no time limit, you get your full results at the end, and nothing you do leaves this device.
