Does the AI forecast earn its place?

A confectionery chain with eight stores and twelve products forecasts next week's sales in six ways, from "same as last week" to an AI model with planners' overrides on top. Each step is judged by how much it cuts forecast error compared with the step before it: its forecast value added. Change the world and the people, and see where the AI is worth it.

Settings

The chain, its stores' sales and the planners are synthetic: generated in this page from a random seed. Festival weeks follow an invented three-year calendar in which Chinese New Year, Hari Raya and Deepavali move from year to year, as they do in real life.
